About G.R.A.C.E PORTAL

Empowering the CCMS Extension Office with a centralized and organized mechanism for managing extension projects throughout their lifecycle.

Bridging Institutions & Communities

The CCMS Extension Office manages extension activities that connect the institution with communities, organizations, and partner agencies through Skills Enhancement Training, Consultancy Services, System Development and Implementation, and other community-oriented extension services.

G.R.A.C.E PORTAL provides a centralized mechanism for recording, updating, and monitoring project proposals, approval status, funding, beneficiaries, project teams, activities, schedules, deliverables, implementation progress, documentation, reports, and post-implementation results.

Extension Services & System Features

Comprehensive tools to support every phase of the extension project lifecycle.

Proposal Management

Record, track, and manage extension project proposals with applicable evaluation and approval status throughout the proposal lifecycle.

Approval Pipeline

Track proposals through Eligibility Check, Technical Evaluation, Head of Extension approval, Defense, Division Office endorsement, and MOA stages.

Funding Status Monitoring

Record and view project funding information including institutional funding, external sources, approved budget, and current funding status.

Participation Tracking

Systematically record and monitor participation of faculty members and students in extension activities across all projects.

Activity & Training

Manage extension activities, training sessions, schedules, venues, participants, deliverables, and implementation information.

Beneficiary Management

Record and organize information about beneficiaries, requesting parties, partner organizations, and their participation in extension activities.

Documentation & Reports

Organize narrative reports, attendance records, photographs, certificates, travel documents, and supporting records for monitoring and evaluation.

Post-Implementation

Record completed activities, implementation results, beneficiary outcomes, client satisfaction, and evaluation data for future planning.
